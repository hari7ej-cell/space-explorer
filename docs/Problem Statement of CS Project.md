**Space explorer – Problem statement**

**Space is a topic that many students and people are curious about, but finding proper and organized information about it can be difficult. There is a lot of information available about planets, space-related missions, astronauts, space images, and other space events, but this information is available on different websites and sources. Because of this, a person who is interested in space may have to search on many different websites to find the information they need.**

**To solve this problem, we are developing a website called Space Explorer. The main aim of this website is to provide useful space-related information in one place, especially for space-curious students and other people who want to learn more about space. The website will collect information from reliable space APIs and present it to users in a simple and structured form.**

**The website will provide information about space-related missions, including their names, purpose, launch details, status, and other available information. Users will also be able to explore information about different planets and understand their important characteristics. Along with this, the website will provide astronaut information, such as their names, missions, and other related details.**

**Another important part of the website will be space images. Users will be able to view images related to planets, missions, astronauts, and other space objects or events. This will make the information more interesting and easier to understand instead of showing only text.**

**The information on the website will be presented in a structured form, so users can easily find and understand what they are looking for. A search option will also be provided to search for a particular planet, mission, astronaut, or other space-related information.**

**The main purpose of Space Explorer is to create a single platform where space-curious students and people can easily explore space-related information. Instead of searching through different websites, users can find information about planets, missions, astronauts, images, and recent space updates in one organized and user-friendly website.**
