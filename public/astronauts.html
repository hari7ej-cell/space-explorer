ABOUT THE DATA

Records first. Sources always.

Wikidata is used to discover people whose occupations are labelled astronaut, cosmonaut or spaceflight participant. Biographies and portraits are enriched from Wikipedia when an English article is available. This is a community-maintained source, so the result is not a guarantee of every person who has ever flown to space.
